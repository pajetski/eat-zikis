# Ziki’s Brand Guide

## Brand core

**Full name:** Ziki’s Greek Street Eats  
**Positioning:** Modern Greek street food with a sunny San Diego point of view.  
**Primary line:** Greek food, with sunshine.  
**Proof points:** Organic ingredients · No seed oils · San Diego pop-ups & farmers markets  

Ziki’s should feel generous, fresh, confident, and polished—never fussy or overly traditional. The identity balances the elegance of the original black-and-white wordmark with the color, ease, and appetite appeal of a coastal pop-up.

## Founder story

Ziki’s was founded by Alyssa Gosselin, whose approach to food is grounded in movement, meal preparation, and ongoing dietary research. She creates fresh, nourishing “power food” with the discipline of an athlete and the warmth of a host. Her curated pop-ups bring that point of view to local businesses and gatherings throughout San Diego.

## Visual identity

### Core palette

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Charcoal | `#14130F` | Wordmark, primary type, dark panels |
| Warm ivory | `#F4EDDC` | Primary brand background |
| Paper | `#FBF7ED` | Light page background |
| Aegean blue | `#0D4596` | Primary accent and calls to action |
| Coastal blue | `#77A7D8` | Secondary accent |
| Olive | `#66713B` | Ingredient and packaging accent |
| Tomato | `#DC4B2C` | Small, energetic highlights only |

### Typography

- **Display:** Georgia or another high-contrast editorial serif. Use for headlines, the wordmark, and short emotional statements.
- **Body:** Geist, Arial, or another clear modern sans serif. Use for paragraphs, navigation, and buttons.
- **Utility:** Geist Mono or a compact monospaced face. Use sparingly for location lines, menu numbers, and small labels.

### Graphic language

- Use warm ivory as the dominant ground; reserve charcoal and Aegean blue for high-impact moments.
- Favor generous margins, asymmetric editorial layouts, thin rules, small tracked labels, and circular stamps.
- Greek-key patterns can appear as a border or texture, but should never compete with the food.
- Keep the original wordmark black-and-white when used as a formal logo. The broader identity supplies the color around it.

## Logo guidance

- Give the logo clear space equal to the height of the small “Greek Street Eats” line.
- Use the black logo on warm ivory or white whenever possible.
- On charcoal, use a clean reversed-white version.
- Do not stretch, outline, add shadows, or place the logo over busy food photography.
- Use the full “Ziki’s Greek Street Eats” lockup for first impressions. The “Z” monogram may be used for small stamps and social avatars once a final vector is produced.

## Photography

Food should look real, abundant, and freshly assembled. Favor late-afternoon natural light, warm stone or linen surfaces, cobalt ceramics, crisp produce, visible herbs, and lively texture. Avoid dark restaurant lighting, overly styled fine-dining plates, generic white studio backgrounds, and plastic-looking ingredients.

## Voice

Ziki’s sounds warm, direct, and lightly playful. Copy should be short, sensory, and confident.

**Use:**

- “Greek food, with sunshine.”
- “Good food. Good mood. Kali orexi.”
- “A little Greece, close to home.”
- “Meet your new favorites.”
- “Power food, made personal.”
- “Let us host something unforgettable.”

**Avoid:**

- Over-explaining Greek culture
- Wellness clichés
- Aggressive “guilt-free” language
- Generic phrases such as “authentic flavors” without a concrete proof point

## Social content system

Use a repeatable mix of:

1. **Crave:** tight food images with a short headline.
2. **Place:** clear pop-up details with strong date, time, and location hierarchy.
3. **People:** the founder, partners, markets, and the energy around the stand.
4. **Proof:** ingredients, preparation, and the no-seed-oils standard.

Keep the logo small on food-first posts. Use Aegean blue and warm ivory for event cards so the feed develops a recognizable rhythm.
